# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone in M3's issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other project space, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected

- Be respectful to people, including when you disagree with their code.
- Criticize the work, not the person who wrote it.
- Assume people are acting in good faith. Ask before deciding they aren't.
- Accept that maintainers will sometimes say no, and that they will explain why.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Comments that demean people for their identity, background or experience.
- Threats, or encouraging others to harass someone.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Continuing to contact someone after they have asked you to stop.

## Reporting

Email [hello@sineframe.com](mailto:hello@sineframe.com) with what happened,
where, and links if you have them. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
private. If a maintainer is involved, say so and they will be left out of
handling it.

## What happens next

Maintainers decide what to do based on what happened. That can be a private
warning, removing comments or code, a temporary ban from the project, or a
permanent one. Serious cases can skip straight to a ban.
