# Fieldnotes privacy policy

To be published at https://fieldnotes.example/privacy on launch day.

## Data the connector accesses

- Notebook ids and names.
- Note ids, titles and full text.

The connector reads and returns only these fields, and only for the
workspace you connect. It doesn't access your account profile, contacts,
location or payment details.

## Purpose

The data is used only to answer your request: to search, show, create,
replace or delete the notes you ask about.

## Recipients

Data is shared only with the AI assistant you connected, to answer your
request. We don't sell it or use it for advertising or model training.

## Retention

The connector stores nothing of its own. Notes stay in your workspace until
you delete them.

## Your controls

Disconnect the connector at any time. Delete notes in Fieldnotes or with
`delete_note`.
