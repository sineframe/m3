# Ledgerly privacy policy

Published at http://ledgerly.example/privacy.

## What we collect

- Your name and email address.
- Your customers' business names and billing email addresses.
- Invoice amounts, currencies and due dates.

## How we use it

We use this data to create and send invoices on your behalf.

## Retention

We keep invoice records for seven years.
